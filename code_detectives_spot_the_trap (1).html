The 5 Red Flags Guide

Spot these 5 clues to catch cyber-tricksters!

1

Too Good to Be True

Promises of 10,000 FREE gems, free consoles, or secret rare skins. Real companies don't give away huge prizes on random pop-ups.

2

Rush or Panic

Countdown clocks (00:15) or scary warnings ("Your computer has 12 viruses!") designed to make you panic and click without thinking.

3

Tricky URLs

Look-alike web addresses such as netfIix-verify-help.com (using a capital I instead of an l) or strange .xyz links.

4

Spelling Mistakes

Sloppy spelling like "WARNNING! Downlaod now!" Real tech and game companies carefully proofread their messages.

5

Asking for Secrets

Anyone asking for your password, home address, phone number, or credit card info. Real moderators NEVER ask for your password.
